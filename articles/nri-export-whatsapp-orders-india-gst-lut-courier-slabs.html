NRI & Export WhatsApp Orders from India: Zero-Rated GST LUT Billing, International Courier Slabs, and Direct wa.me Closing Scripts

November 20, 2026 • 9 min read • 1808 words • Plain-English Playbook

A Jaipur block-print studio gets a WhatsApp message at 10:15 PM IST from a buyer in Dubai: "Need two hand-embroidered chanderi kurta sets for a wedding next Friday. Can you ship to Jumeirah and share the final price in USD?" The order value is ₹8,500 ($100). Most Indian D2C sellers handle this inquiry the same way they handle a domestic order from Pune: they quote an 18% GST-inclusive price, guess a flat courier fee, and paste a raw 10-digit phone number that breaks on international WhatsApp. Within three messages, the buyer goes silent, or the seller eats a ₹1,530 tax lockup and a ₹3,200 express courier bill that wipes out the entire profit.

Cross-border WhatsApp selling does not need an expensive Shopify Plus store or a dedicated export desk. It needs three tight operational numbers: zero-rated GST billing under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT), weight-slab discipline between India Post International Tracked Packet and private express couriers, and clean wa.me deep links with pre-filled currency and customs disclosures. When you get those three right, a ₹8,500 ($100) ethnic wear or handicraft parcel retains a clean 45% net margin (₹3,825) instead of bleeding cash into blocked working capital.

1. The ₹8,500 ($100) Order Math: GST LUT Zero-Rating vs. 18% IGST Lockup

Under Section 16 of the IGST Act, physical goods shipped outside India qualify as zero-rated supplies. You have two legal ways to invoice an NRI or overseas buyer:

  • Option A — Export with Payment of IGST: You charge 18% IGST (₹1,530 on an ₹8,500 handicraft or embroidered garment order, or 12% on certain apparel slabs above ₹1,000), deposit ₹1,530 with the government in your monthly GSTR-3B, and wait 60 to 120 days for the customs ICEGATE refund scroll to credit your bank account.
  • Option B — Export under Bond / Letter of Undertaking (LUT Form GST RFD-11): You file Form GST RFD-11 once per financial year on the GST portal (free, instant ARN approval, no bank guarantee required unless you have a prior tax prosecution). Every export invoice carries 0% IGST with the mandatory declaration: "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of integrated tax."

Look at what happens to your cash flow on a single ₹8,500 ($100) order, and across 40 NRI orders during a festive quarter:

Line Item (₹8,500 / $100 Order) With GST LUT (0% IGST) Without LUT (18% IGST Lockup)
Product Selling Price (FOB / Ex-Works) ₹8,500 ₹8,500
IGST Charged / Blocked on Invoice ₹0 ₹1,530 (locked 60–120 days)
COGS (Fabric, Craft, Artisans, Packaging) ₹3,225 (37.9%) ₹3,225 (37.9%)
India Post International Tracked Packet (1 kg) ₹1,450 ₹1,450

That ₹61,200 difference across 40 orders is enough to fund an entire seasonal fabric run. Worse, if you ship via informal courier without a proper Shipping Bill or Postal Bill of Export (PBE-1 / PBE-2), you cannot even claim the ₹1,530 IGST refund later—turning a temporary cash lockup into a permanent 18% loss.

When generating the commercial invoice for an overseas WhatsApp buyer, never send a domestic retail slip with CGST and SGST split lines. Use BillFold to set tax to 0% (Export under LUT), switch the currency symbol to $ or keep ₹ with the INR-USD conversion rate noted in the footer, and include your LUT ARN number, 8-digit HSN code, and the buyer's full destination postal code.

2. International Courier Slabs: ₹1,450 India Post Tracked Packet vs. Private Express

Shipping cost is where most Indian sellers misprice NRI orders. A handloom saree, two kurta sets, or a brass idol in corrugated export packaging typically weighs between 750 g and 1.2 kg—or bills at 1.5 kg volumetric weight if packed in a loose gift box. Before you quote a shipping fee on WhatsApp, run your box dimensions (L × W × H cm ÷ 5000) through the MarginMint Volumetric Weight Helper so an oversized box does not jump you into the next slab.

Look at how the margin math shifts on our ₹8,500 ($100) order if you pick the wrong carrier:

  • With India Post International Tracked Packet at ₹1,450: Total revenue is ₹8,500. Subtract ₹3,225 COGS and ₹1,450 shipping. Your net profit is ₹3,825, which is exactly 45.0% net margin. (If you collect $100 product + $18 shipping = $118, your net profit stays ₹3,825 while your product-only margin climbs even higher.)
  • With Private Express at ₹3,400 (absorbed into "Free Worldwide Shipping"): Total revenue is ₹8,500. Subtract ₹3,225 COGS and ₹3,400 express freight. Your net profit collapses to ₹1,875 (22.1%)—and if the buyer's volumetric box hits 1.5 kg, express freight jumps to ₹4,150, leaving you with just ₹1,125 (13.2%) before payment gateway fees.

Three Rules for Export Packaging That Protect Your Slab

  1. Keep dead weight under 950 g for single-garment orders: Use 3-ply high-burst E-flute mailers or double-layered poly-bubble export pouches instead of heavy 5-ply master cartons when shipping textiles. Saving 220 g of cardboard keeps you inside the 1 kg slab.
  2. Always file the Postal Bill of Export (PBE) at Dak Ghar Niryat Kendra (DNK): If you ship through India Post's DNK portal, you get an electronic PBE, barcoded customs CN22/CN23 declaration, and end-to-end tracking without paying a private customs broker ₹1,200 per airway bill.
  3. State DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid) clearly before payment: Overseas buyers in the UK, EU, and Canada occasionally face local VAT or import duty at the doorstep. Disclosing "Prices exclude destination country import duties, if any" in writing on WhatsApp prevents chargebacks and angry 1-star reviews.

3. Fixing the Broken International wa.me Link Problem

Indian sellers who run Instagram ads or post catalog Reels for both Mumbai buyers and NRI buyers in Dubai, New York, London, and Singapore hit a recurring technical snag: country code errors in WhatsApp links. Either the seller forgets the 91 prefix (making https://wa.me/9876543210 fail for overseas callers), or a staff member pastes 91 twice (https://wa.me/91919876543210), or an international buyer replies from a +971, +1, +44, or +65 number and the team formats follow-up links incorrectly.

We built the Country Code Quick-Switch directly into TapPing to eliminate that friction. Pick +91 India, +971 UAE, +1 US, +44 UK, or +65 SG beside the phone field, and TapPing automatically normalizes local digits, strips leading zeros, and prevents double-prefixing if you paste a full international number. You also get an instant QR code and a source tag (such as IG-NRI-DIWALI) so you know which Reel or catalog post drove the export inquiry.

Before you paste that link into your Instagram bio or YouTube Shorts description, run your bio line and link CTA through CopyCraft to make sure the callout fits cleanly above the mobile fold without getting truncated at 150 characters.

Overseas buyers do not want five paragraphs of small talk. They want three facts in the first message: exact landed price in their currency, carrier and delivery window, and how to pay safely right now. Copy and adapt these four scripts inside TapPing.

Script 1: Initial Catalog Inquiry (Pre-Filled Inbound wa.me Message)

Reply TRACKED or EXPRESS and share your full street address + postal code so I can send your payment link and 0% GST export invoice.

5. Pre-Dispatch Export Checklist for Indian WhatsApp Sellers

Run through this five-point check before handing any international WhatsApp parcel to the courier:

  • GST LUT Active: Confirm your FY 2026–27 Form GST RFD-11 ARN is active on the GST portal and printed on your BillFold invoice.
  • HSN & Country of Origin: Verify the 8-digit HSN code (for example, 6204 for women's cotton/silk ensembles or 7418 for brass handicrafts) and Country of Origin: India appear on both the invoice and the CN23 customs label.
  • Volumetric Weight Check: Measure the sealed parcel in MarginMint before quoting freight so a 1.1 kg box does not get billed as 2 kg.
  • Payment Reconciliation: Save the bank FIRC / payment gateway settlement advice alongside the shipping bill or PBE so your CA can close zero-rated export compliance cleanly at quarter-end.
  • TapPing Link Hygiene: Keep separate ?text= source tags in TapPing for domestic vs. NRI campaigns so your team immediately spots overseas inquiries and quotes LUT + international courier slabs from message one.
