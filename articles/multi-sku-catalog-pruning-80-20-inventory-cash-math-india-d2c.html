Multi-SKU Catalog Pruning & 80/20 Inventory Cash Math for Indian D2C Brands

November 21, 2026 • 9 min read • 1760 words • Plain-English Playbook
WATCH OUR ORIGINAL AI-NARRATED WALKTHROUGH

Walk into the warehouse of almost any Indian D2C brand doing ₹12 lakh to ₹30 lakh in monthly revenue and you will see the same silent cash trap. Four products pay the rent, salaries, and Meta ad bills. Eight other products sit in neat cardboard cartons on the top shelf, collecting dust and quietly draining your bank account every single week.

Most founders believe a wider catalog makes their storefront look like a real brand. In practice, carrying 12 SKUs when only 4 sell consistently cuts your cash conversion cycle in half and forces your profitable winners to subsidize your losers. If you want to fix cash flow without raising outside capital, you do not need another ad agency. You need to run an honest 80/20 catalog audit.

1. The 12-SKU vs. 4-Hero-SKU Cash Math

Let us put real Indian D2C numbers on the table. Suppose your brand sells personal care, footwear, or home essentials and generates ₹4,50,000 in monthly gross contribution profit across 12 active SKUs before ad-testing experiments.

When you export your last 90 days of Shopify or WooCommerce order lines and deduct COGS, GST, packaging, forward freight, and COD Return-to-Origin (RTO) losses using MarginMint, the split almost always looks like this:

  • Top 4 Hero SKUs: Generate 78% of your monthly cash profit, which equals ₹3,51,000 per month (an average of ₹87,750 per SKU). Inventory turns every 22 to 28 days.
  • Bottom 8 Slow-Moving SKUs: Generate only 22% of monthly cash profit, which equals ₹99,000 per month (an average of ₹12,375 per SKU). Inventory sits for 95 to 140 days.

That 78/22 split looks manageable on a revenue spreadsheet until you add the two hidden costs that never show up on your Shopify dashboard: trapped working capital and zombie ad-testing spend.

Hero Tier (Top 33%) 4 SKUs ₹3,51,000 (78%) ₹0 (Proven Winners) ₹3,51,000 ₹1,95,000 (24-day turn)

Look closely at those 8 slow-moving SKUs. Together, they tie up ₹1,80,000 in dead or slow-moving inventory (an average of ₹22,500 per SKU sitting in boxes). At the same time, your marketing team spends ₹28,000 every month testing new creatives, dynamic catalog carousels, and broad Meta ad sets trying to "fix" those 8 products.

Once you subtract that ₹28,000 ad-testing burn from the ₹99,000 gross profit of the 8 laggards, the entire slow tier leaves you with just ₹71,000 across 8 different supply chains—or ₹8,875 per SKU per month. Worse, two of those eight SKUs are usually cash-negative after accounting for COD RTO.

2. Why Slow SKUs Quietly Destroy Your Contribution Margin

A slow SKU is never neutral. Even when it sits untouched in your godown, it creates four compounding operational taxes on your business:

A. The COD RTO & Volumetric Weight Trap

Slow-moving items are often impulse-buy variants, odd sizes, or bulky gift sets. When an item has a high volumetric weight (Length × Width × Height ÷ 5000) and low repeat demand, courier slabs eat your margin alive. If a ₹799 slow SKU ships in a box billed at 1 kg volumetric weight (₹115 forward + ₹95 reverse on RTO) and suffers a 26% COD return rate, each failed parcel burns ₹210 plus ₹30 packaging—wiping out the profit of two delivered orders.

Before keeping any secondary SKU in your catalog, plug its exact box dimensions, GST slab, and realistic RTO rate into MarginMint and click + Save SKU in the Saved SKU Scenarios bar. Save your top 4 hero SKUs and compare them side by side against your bottom 4 SKUs. You will spot the margin losers in under three minutes.

B. Ad Algorithm Dilution

Meta Advantage+ and Google Performance Max algorithms optimize for total conversion volume. When you feed a 12-SKU catalog into a single shopping campaign with a ₹3,000 daily budget, the algorithm spends ₹900/day exploring clicks on low-converting variants just to gather data. That ₹28,000 monthly exploration tax starves your 4 hero SKUs of the impression share they need to lower your blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

C. Packaging, Picking Errors & Customer Support Overhead

Every extra SKU requires its own barcode sticker, inner box size, product photography, and FAQ script. When your packing table handles 12 SKUs instead of 4, wrong-variant dispatches rise, causing avoidable exchange shipments and angry WhatsApp messages.

D. Supplier MOQ Lock-In

Indian contract manufacturers and garment units rarely accept 50-unit reorders. To keep a slow SKU "in stock," founders routinely place 250-unit minimum orders at ₹180/unit—locking up ₹45,000 on a single variant that takes four months to clear.

3. The 4-Metric Catalog Audit: Keep, Bundle, or Kill

Do not prune your catalog on gut feeling. Export your last 90 days of SKU data and score every product against four hard thresholds:

  1. Net Profit After RTO (Target: ≥ 22%): Calculate true delivered unit economics in MarginMint. Any SKU under 15% net margin after inclusive GST and realistic COD RTO cannot survive paid traffic.
  2. Inventory Turnover Days (Target: ≤ 45 days): Divide current stock value by average monthly COGS sold, then multiply by 30. Anything above 75 days is trapped cash.
  3. 30-Day Ad-Free Organic Velocity (Target: ≥ 15% of orders): Does anyone buy this SKU through direct search, repeat WhatsApp orders, or organic checkout upsells when you turn off its ads?
  4. COD Return Rate (Target: ≤ 18%): High-RTO SKUs above 25% signal a mismatch between your ad promise and the physical product in the box.

Based on those four numbers, sort all 12 SKUs into three buckets:

  • Bucket 1 — Keep & Scale (4 Hero SKUs): High margin (≥ 25%), fast turn (≤ 35 days), low RTO (≤ 16%). Give these 4 SKUs 100% of your paid ad budget.
  • Bucket 2 — Bundle Only (3 Complementary SKUs): Good margin on paper, slow standalone velocity. Stop running standalone ads for them. Turn them into prepaid checkout add-ons or "Buy 2 Get 1" combo packs to raise Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Bucket 3 — Liquidate & Kill (5 Dead SKUs): Low margin, high RTO, or 100+ days of stock. Stop reordering immediately and convert the ₹1,80,000 dead stock back into liquid cash within 21 days.

4. How to Liquidate ₹1,80,000 in Dead Stock Without Hurting Brand Trust

Never run a 50% off clearance banner on your main storefront homepage. Heavy sitewide discounting trains your best customers to wait for sales and ruins your paid ROAS.

Instead, run a quiet, high-trust VIP WhatsApp Clearance & Bundle Drive aimed strictly at past buyers who already trust your quality:

Step 1: Build a Direct UPI-First WhatsApp Clearance Link

Past customers convert fastest on WhatsApp because there is zero checkout friction. Use TapPing to generate a pre-filled WhatsApp order link and printable QR code for your warehouse clearance pack—for example, a 3-unit combo at 25% off prepaid via UPI, with free shipping included.

Here is a plain-English WhatsApp broadcast template that converts without sounding desperate:

"Hi [Name], we are clearing our final batch of [SKU Name] before our December restock. Because you ordered from us earlier, you can grab a 2-pack for ₹999 prepaid (regular ₹1,398) with free 48-hour dispatch. Only 34 sets left—reply YES or tap here to lock yours via UPI."

Selling ₹1,80,000 of trapped inventory at a blended 25% clearance discount recovers ₹1,35,000 in immediate liquid cash. Even after ₹12,000 in courier and packaging costs, you free up ₹1,23,000 in bank balance in three weeks instead of waiting six months.

Step 2: Issue Clean GST Clearance Invoices

When liquidating bundled or discounted stock to B2B resellers, local boutiques, or bulk corporate gifting buyers, your paperwork must stay 100% compliant. Use BillFold to generate clean GST tax invoices with item-level discount lines, accurate HSN codes, and CGST/SGST or IGST splits so your monthly GSTR-1 reconciliation matches your bank statement to the rupee.

Step 3: Clean Up Product Pages & Storefront Copy

Once a dead SKU is sold out, do not leave broken 404 links or "Out of Stock" badges cluttering your collection pages. Redirect retired product URLs (301 redirect) to your closest hero SKU, and rewrite your collection titles and hero product descriptions using CopyCraft so every remaining page leads with crisp benefits, exact specifications, and clean SERP meta tags.
