2-Pack & 3-Pack D2C Bundling Math in India: Lifting ₹799 Single-SKU Orders from 9% Loss to 27% Net Profit

November 26, 2026 • 8 min read • 1686 words • Plain-English Playbook
WATCH OUR ORIGINAL AI-NARRATED WALKTHROUGH

Most Indian D2C founders do not have a traffic problem. They have an order-value problem disguised as a Meta Ads problem.

Take a classic entry-level Indian consumer SKU: a ₹799 bottle of hair serum, face wash, or daily nutrition powder. Your landed manufacturing cost (COGS) is ₹210. On paper, selling a ₹210 product for ₹799 looks like a comfortable business. You tell yourself the gross margin is 74%, launch Meta Advantage+ shopping campaigns, and watch orders roll in at a ₹180 cost per acquisition (CPA).

Then thirty days later, your bank balance is lower than when you started. When you plug the real numbers into MarginMint—factoring inclusive 18% GST, ₹75 forward courier, ₹35 corrugated box and tape, 2% payment gateway or COD handling charges, 18% GST on your Meta Ads invoice, a 15% COD RTO rate, and ₹83 per order in fixed warehouse and team overhead—that ₹799 single-SKU order is actually losing roughly ₹72 per delivered order (-9.0% net margin).

The fix is rarely cutting your Meta CPA in half. You cannot force Meta auctions to give you ₹80 clicks in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities during competitive months. The fix is changing what sits inside the courier polybag: shifting buyers from a ₹799 1-Pack into a ₹1,399 2-Pack or a ₹1,899 3-Pack that shares the exact same shipping label, the same corrugated box, and the same ₹180 ad click.

Why the ₹799 Single-SKU Order Bleeds 9% in India

To see why ₹799 breaks down, you have to stop calculating profit on MRP and start calculating cash left per delivered order. In India, three silent deductions eat a low-ticket order before you ever pay yourself:

  • GST is extracted from the selling price, not added on top: At 18% GST inclusive on ₹799, your actual ex-GST revenue is ₹799 ÷ 1.18 = ₹677.12. The remaining ₹121.88 belongs to the GST portal.
  • Meta Ads invoices carry an extra 18% GST: A ₹180 CPA in Ads Manager is ₹212.40 deducted from your HDFC or ICICI current account. When 15% of placed orders return as RTO, you spend ₹212.40 ÷ 0.85 = ₹249.88 in ad cash per delivered order.
  • One courier box costs the same whether it holds one bottle or three: A 350g parcel and an 850g parcel in a standard 20×15×8 cm box both sit inside the same 500g–1kg courier slab, costing roughly ₹75 forward shipping plus ₹35 for the box, honeycomb wrap, and barcode label.

Add ₹210 COGS, ₹51 in payment/COD collection fees (2% + ₹35 COD fee), ₹32.65 in pro-rated RTO forward-and-reverse courier burn across failed orders, ₹12 in damaged RTO packaging write-offs, and ₹83.33 in fixed monthly overhead (₹25,000 fixed rent and software spread over 300 orders). Total cost per delivered ₹799 order reaches ₹870.72—leaving a -₹71.72 (-9.0%) loss on every single bottle you celebrate shipping.

Every fixed rupee—the ₹110 box and shipping, the ₹249.88 ad acquisition cost, the ₹44.65 RTO logistics burn, and the ₹83.33 overhead—stays flat while revenue climbs by ₹1,100.

The 3-Step Bundle Pricing Formula for Indian D2C Stores

Guessing bundle prices like "Buy 2 Get 10% Off" leaves money on the table or breaks your shipping slab. Use this three-step rule instead:

1. Check Volumetric Weight Before Setting the 3-Pack Price

Courier aggregators (Shiprocket, Delhivery, Blue Dart, Xpressbees) bill the higher of dead weight or volumetric weight, calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000. Three 100ml bottles packed side-by-side in a 20×15×8 cm box have a volumetric weight of 0.48 kg—just under the 500g slab, keeping forward shipping at ₹75. If your 3-Pack forces a taller 22×18×12 cm box, volumetric weight jumps to 0.95 kg, pushing you into the 1 kg slab (₹115–₹130). Always measure the 3-Pack box in the MarginMint Volumetric Weight Helper before locking your MRP.

2. Price the 2-Pack as Your Anchor, Not Your Best Deal

If 1 unit is ₹799, pricing the 2-Pack at ₹1,399 saves the customer ₹199 (roughly ₹700 per unit). Pricing the 3-Pack at ₹1,899 drops the per-unit price to ₹633—saving ₹498 off the 3-unit MRP. That ₹500 gap between ₹1,399 and ₹1,899 makes the 3-Pack look obvious to repeat buyers and household shoppers, lifting your blended AOV above ₹1,350 without raising your Meta bid.

3. Pair Bundles with a Prepaid UPI Nudge to Cut RTO

Higher-ticket COD orders carry higher buyer remorse at the doorstep. If a ₹1,899 COD parcel returns as RTO, you do not just lose ₹150 in forward and reverse courier fees—you also tie up ₹630 of inventory for 14 days. Offer a flat ₹75–₹100 Prepaid UPI Instant Discount on 2-Pack and 3-Pack orders (₹1,799 on UPI vs ₹1,899 on COD). Because you save the ₹35 COD collection fee and eliminate the 15% RTO risk, a ₹1,799 prepaid 3-Pack puts more cash in your bank than a ₹1,899 COD order.

Two Bundle Traps That Quietly Kill Margin in India

Even with strong AOV, two common mistakes wipe out bundle gains for first-time D2C operators:

  • Mixed-GST "Mixed Supply" Combos: Under Section 8 of the CGST Act, if you bundle a 5% GST item (like dry fruits or herbal tea) with an 18% GST item (like a ceramic mug or shaker bottle) for a single combo price, the entire bundle attracts the highest GST rate (18%). Keep your 2-Pack and 3-Pack bundles within the same HSN code or same GST slab, or itemize them separately on your BillFold tax invoice so you do not overpay 13% GST on the lower-tax item.
  • Heavy "Free Gift" Add-Ons: Adding a free 250g glass jar or metal roller to a ₹1,399 2-Pack looks generous in ad creatives, but if that gift pushes parcel weight from 480g to 620g, your courier immediately bills the 1 kg slab (+₹45 forward and +₹45 reverse on RTO) plus ₹60 in extra COGS. Stick to same-SKU multipacks or lightweight sachets under 30g.

How This Shows Up in Your 90-Day Bank Balance

Unit margin is only half the story. When you sell 600 orders a month at ₹799 (-₹71.72 net per order), you burn ₹43,032 every month in operating cash—before even counting the 6-to-9 day COD remittance lag from courier partners.

Shift 60% of those 600 monthly orders into ₹1,399 2-Packs and ₹1,899 3-Packs (say 240 single units, 210 two-packs, and 150 three-packs at blended 18% GST):

  • 240 × 1-Pack: 240 × (-₹71.72) = -₹17,213
  • 210 × 2-Pack: 210 × (+₹214.75) = +₹45,098
  • 150 × 3-Pack: 150 × (+₹418.48) = +₹62,772
  • Total Monthly Net Profit: +₹90,657/month on the exact same 600 ad conversions and the exact same ₹1,08,000 Meta ad spend (+GST).

Model that shift across a full 90-day cash cycle in CashPulse to see how faster prepaid UPI settlements and higher contribution per box eliminate mid-month working-capital crunches when vendor restocks and GSTR-3B challans fall due.

Updating Your Storefront, Invoices, and WhatsApp Flows

Once your bundle math works, wire the offer cleanly across your storefront and post-purchase stack:

  • Product Page Copy & Title Hygiene: Rewrite your bundle selector labels and meta descriptions in CopyCraft so the per-unit savings ("₹633/bottle • 3-Month Supply • Free Shipping") sit inside the first 120 pixels on mobile without tripping spammy discount flags.
  • GST-Compliant Bundle Invoicing: When you sell a 3-Pack at ₹1,899, generate the customer tax invoice in BillFold with the correct HSN code, quantity = 3 (or combo SKU), and proper CGST/SGST or IGST split based on Place of Supply so your GSTR-1 B2CS summary reconciles cleanly.
  • WhatsApp COD Confirmation & Upsell Links: Before dispatching a single-SKU ₹799 COD order, send an automated order-confirmation message built in TapPing offering a one-tap upgrade to the 2-Pack for ₹550 extra ("Add a second bottle for ₹550—zero extra shipping"). Even a 12% upgrade rate turns a negative-margin ad campaign into a cash-generating funnel.

Weekly Bundle Audit Checklist

  1. Pull your last 30 days of Shopify/WooCommerce orders by line-item count: Calculate the exact percentage of 1-unit vs 2-unit vs 3-unit orders.
  2. Run all three tiers through MarginMint: Set Units / Bundle Qty to 1, 2, and 3 while keeping your actual Unit COGS, box cost, and Meta CPA constant.
  3. Check your 3-Pack volumetric slab: Confirm your 3-unit carton stays under 0.50 kg (or 1.00 kg) so courier surcharges do not eat the bundle lift.
  4. Set your default cart selector to the 2-Pack: Make the 2-Pack pre-selected on mobile product pages and highlight the 3-Pack as "Best Value (Save ₹498)" with a ₹75–₹100 Prepaid UPI incentive.
  5. Verify 90-day cash runway in CashPulse: Ensure your vendor inventory reorder cycle covers the higher unit volume (1.8x–2.2x units per order) before scaling Meta spend.
