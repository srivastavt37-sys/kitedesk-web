Inventory Turns & Dead-Stock Liquidation in India: Turning 3× Slow SKUs into ₹2.7 Lakh Fresh Working Capital

November 27, 2026 • 9 min read • 1942 words • Plain-English Playbook

Most Indian D2C founders check their bank balance every morning and wonder why a profitable brand feels broke. You are shipping orders, your Meta ROAS looks decent on the dashboard, and your GST filings show steady monthly turnover. Yet when a supplier asks for a ₹2.5 lakh advance on your best-selling SKU, the current account is short.

The missing cash is rarely lost in ads or courier bills alone. In nine out of ten small warehouses across Surat, Jaipur, Bengaluru, and Delhi NCR, that cash is sitting inside cardboard cartons on the back rack. You bought 12 SKUs, 8 of them move every week, and 4 of them sit untouched for months.

This playbook walks through the exact rupee math of a ₹9,00,000 (₹9 lakh) warehouse running 12 SKUs at a sluggish 3.0× annual inventory turn, and shows how cutting 4 slow movers through a 15% UPI bundle clearance recovers ₹2,70,300 (₹2.7 lakh) in fresh working capital while lifting blended turns to 8.4×.

1. Why Annual Inventory Turns Matter More Than Gross Margin

Gross margin tells you how much profit sits inside one sold unit. Inventory turns tell you how many times your invested rupee comes back into your bank account in twelve months. A product with a 55% gross margin that turns twice a year earns less cash per rupee invested than a product with a 42% gross margin that turns nine times a year.

The formula is straightforward and takes ten seconds inside StockPulse:

Annual Inventory Turns = (Daily Unit Sales × 365) / Current On-Hand Stock (or at the warehouse level, Annual COGS / Average Inventory Value at Cost).

Here is how to read your number on Indian D2C shelves:

  • Below 4.0× (Slow Capital): Stock sits in your warehouse for more than 91 days. Storage rent, dust, carton damage, expiry risk, and 18%–24% working-capital interest quietly eat your margin.
  • 4.0× to 12.0× (Healthy Turns): You hold 30 to 90 days of cover. Supplier lead times are respected without locking up six months of cash.
  • Above 12.0× (Fast-Mover): Stock cycles in under 30 days. Your risk here is not dead cash; it is a sudden stockout during festive spikes or supplier delays.

2. The ₹9 Lakh Warehouse Breakdown: 12 SKUs at 3.0× Turns

Let us look at a realistic Indian home-and-lifestyle or personal-care D2C brand holding ₹9,00,000 in inventory at landed cost across 12 SKUs. Over the past 12 months, total Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) across all 12 SKUs was ₹27,00,000.

Divide ₹27,00,000 by ₹9,00,000 and you get 3.0× annual turns. That means an average rupee spent on stock takes 121.7 days (four full months) to come back.

When you split those 12 SKUs into winners and laggards, the trap becomes obvious:

Meanwhile, even the 8 core SKUs are slightly over-stocked at 86.5 days of cover (4.2× turns) because the founder ordered large 90-day batches to chase a 4% supplier volume discount. That 4% supplier discount feels smart on a purchase order, until you calculate what 45 extra days of idle inventory does to your cashflow inside CashPulse.

3. The Hidden Carrying Cost of Holding Slow Stock

Many founders refuse to discount dead stock because they do not want to "book a loss" on paper. They tell themselves, "It doesn't expire for two years, we will sell it at full MRP eventually."

That thinking ignores four real rupee drains happening every month those cartons sit on your rack:

  1. Working Capital Cost (18%–24% p.a.): Whether you borrow on an overdraft limit or miss out on scaling a winning ad set, ₹3,18,000 locked at 20% opportunity cost burns ₹5,300 every month (₹63,600/year).
  2. Warehouse Rack & Handling Rent: At ₹35–₹55 per sq. ft. in a metro 3PL or self-managed godown, slow cartons take up 35% of your shelf space—roughly ₹4,500/month in wasted storage and bin fees.
  3. Shrinkage, Dust & Packaging Decay: Labels fade, outer mono-cartons get crushed during stock counts, and GST input credit sits idle.
  4. Hero Stockout Losses: Because ₹3.18 lakh is frozen in slow SKUs, you delay reordering your top 3 sellers by 10 days, losing 150 high-margin orders during peak weekends.

Over 12 months, holding that ₹3,18,000 batch at 0.76× turns quietly costs you ₹1,17,600 in carrying drag—far more than a controlled clearance markdown.

4. The 15% UPI Bundle Clearance: Turning 4 Slow SKUs into ₹2.7 Lakh Cash

Instead of dumping dead stock at 60% off on a liquidation portal—which wrecks your brand perception and eats marketplace commissions—use a 15% UPI-only bundle clearance aimed at your existing buyers and WhatsApp list.

Why 15% on UPI bundles instead of a flat 40% sitewide sale?

  • Zero RTO Bleed: By making the clearance offer UPI-only via TapPing payment links and dynamic QR codes, you eliminate Cash-on-Delivery (COD) returns. On clearance items, COD RTO rates routinely hit 25%–30%, which wipes out recovery cash in ₹140–₹160 forward-plus-reverse shipping fees.
  • Protected Average Order Value (AOV): Bundling 1 slow SKU with 1 fast hero SKU (or selling a 2-pack clearance combo at 15% off) keeps your shipping cost per rupee collected under 7%. Check your exact net contribution margin before launching inside MarginMint.
  • Clean GST Billing: Issue a proper discounted tax invoice through BillFold showing the taxable value after trade discount, so your GSTR-1 and inventory ledger match rupee-for-rupee.

Exact Clearance Math on the ₹3,18,000 Slow-SKU Batch

Let us run the numbers on liquidating the 4 slow SKUs (₹3,18,000 inventory value at cost):

  • Slow-SKU Cost Value: ₹3,18,000 across 1,060 units (average landed cost ₹300/unit).
  • Normal Selling Price (ex-GST): ₹520/unit (42.3% gross margin). Total normal ex-GST value = ₹5,51,200.
  • Clearance Mechanics: Sold in 2-unit or hero-paired bundles at a 15% discount off cost-recovery target or bundled where the slow SKU clears at ₹255 net recovery per unit after incremental courier allocation (a 15% markdown from landed cost, or ~50% off MRP when paired with a full-margin hero item).
  • Gross Cash Recovered on Slow SKUs: 1,060 units × ₹255 net recovery = ₹2,70,300 (₹2.70 lakh) directly into your bank account within 21 days via UPI.
  • Controlled Markdown Absorbed: ₹3,18,000 − ₹2,70,300 = ₹47,700 one-time write-down—less than half of the ₹1,17,600 annual carrying drag you avoid.

Better yet, if you pair the 4 slow SKUs as "Buy 1 Hero SKU at ₹999 + Add Clearance Pack for ₹299 on UPI" (where ₹255 clears the slow unit after ₹44 incremental shipping), your blended order remains profitable on Day 1 while flushing all 1,060 slow units out of the godown.

5. Lifting Blended Warehouse Turns from 3.0× to 8.4×

Clearing the 4 dead-weight SKUs gives you ₹2.7 lakh in cash, but the second half of the jump comes from tightening your reorder cycle on the 8 core SKUs using StockPulse.

Previously, the 8 core SKUs held ₹5,82,000 in inventory (86.5 days of cover) to support ₹24,57,000 in annual COGS (₹6,731.50/day COGS). Why were you holding 86.5 days of cover when your Surat or Bhiwandi vendor delivers in 10 days?

By switching to a 10-day lead time + 14-day safety buffer + 30-day reorder cycle, your average on-hand stock drops from 86.5 days to 43.4 days of cover:

  • Daily COGS on 8 Core SKUs: ₹24,57,000 / 365 = ₹6,731.51/day.
  • New Lean Average Inventory (43.45 days): ₹6,731.51 × 43.45 = ₹2,92,500.
  • New Blended Annual Inventory Turns: ₹24,57,000 / ₹2,92,500 = 8.40× (Healthy Turns).

7. Three Mistakes That Send Warehouses Back to 3× Turns

  • Chasing 5% MOQ discounts on untested variants: Buying 500 units of a new colourway just to save ₹15 per piece is how ₹3 lakh gets stranded on shelf 4. Pay ₹15 more per unit on a 100-unit test batch; once it clears 8× turns, scale the PO.
  • Offering COD on clearance sales: Clearance shoppers are price-sensitive and impulse-driven. If you allow COD on discounted bundles, 25% of parcels bounce back as RTO, turning your cash-recovery drive into a ₹45,000 courier bill.
  • Ignoring reorder triggers on your top 20% SKUs: While clearing slow stock, keep a close eye on your fast-movers (>12× turns) in StockPulse so you never miss the Next PO Order-By Date on the products that actually pay your rent.
