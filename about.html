About KiteDesk

Built to replace bloated SaaS with fast, free utilities.

KiteDesk started with a simple observation: solo founders, local shopkeepers, and independent creators shouldn't have to pay monthly subscriptions or hand over their email just to generate an invoice or a QR code.

Every tool on KiteDesk runs directly in your browser. Your invoice details, customer names, and drafts never leave your device. Alongside the toolkit, we publish daily, no-fluff guides on organic search, local conversion, and lean operations.

KiteDesk is 100% free to use and supported by lightweight display advertising.
